About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Graduate School of AI, KAIST, advised by Prof. Eunho Yang in the Machine Learning and Intelligence Lab.

My research focuses on multimodal reasoning and capability improvement, with the goal of building models that can better understand visual information, diagnose their own failures, and improve through feedback. I have worked on 3D data understanding, mitigating visual hallucinations in large vision-language models, and improving spatial reasoning capabilities. Currently, I am exploring agentic multimodal refinement, where vision-language models evaluate generated videos, diagnose failures, and iteratively improve the generation process based on previous interaction and evaluation history.

Research Interests: Multimodal Reasoning · Visual & Spatial Intelligence · Agentic VLMs
