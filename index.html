About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate at the Graduate School of AI, KAIST, advised by Prof. Eunho Yang in the Machine Learning and Intelligence Lab.

My research interests lie in building multimodal models with stronger visual, spatial, and physical intelligence. I have worked on 3D data understanding, mitigating visual hallucinations in large vision-language models, and improving their spatial reasoning capabilities. Currently, I am exploring how to endow video generation models with physical intelligence, particularly toward generating physically plausible object interactions and dynamics.

3D Data Understanding Visual Hallucination Mitigation Spatial Intelligence in LVLMs Physical Intelligence in Video Generation
